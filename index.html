How it works

From authorization to publication

Every publication remains an explicit user action, with current account capabilities loaded before submission.

  1. ConnectSign in and grant the permissions shown by the selected platform.
  2. PrepareSelect media, write the caption, and choose the destination account.
  3. ReviewConfirm privacy, interaction settings, disclosures, and timing for that post.
  4. PublishSubmit now or schedule the local queue, then monitor platform processing.

Account control

Your authorization stays reversible.

Disconnect TikTok from the application to revoke its saved authorization. You can also remove access in TikTok settings and delete all local application data from your computer.
